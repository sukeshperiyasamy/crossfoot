## 1. THE STRATEGIC JOB OF THIS WEBSITE

The site has exactly **four jobs** — nothing more:

1. **Make the stranger trust you fast.** You are an unknown solo operator asking owners to share financial records. The site must visibly answer the buyer's 6-point evaluation checklist (operational understanding, concrete proof, numerical reliability, no lock-in, data privacy, risk-reversal pilot) before the first call.
2. **Filter, not persuade.** It should attract distributor/manufacturing owners with reconciliation pain and quietly repel everyone else (students, "how to clean my own Excel" searchers, dashboards-for-fun). Traffic quality > traffic volume.
3. **Convert to one action:** book a 15-minute discovery call (Calendly). Secondary action: download the sample exception register (lead capture for not-ready visitors).
4. **Anchor value, not price.** Sell the ₹8,500 fixed-scope diagnostic as a business control outcome — never as "Excel work."

> **Build discipline (from the 30-day plan):** The site is a Week-1 lightweight asset, not a project. Phase 1 is a 5–7 page site shippped in 2–3 working days. Anything bigger waits for validation gates (Section 10). The first milestone is a paying client, not a polished website.

### 4.2 THE DIAGNOSTIC PAGE `/diagnostic` (your money page)

1. **Problem restatement** (2–3 sentences, distributor vocabulary).
2. **What you get — deliverables list:** ① Data health & leakage audit ② Clean consolidated master dataset (up to 2 files / 100,000 rows) ③ **Traceable Excel exception register** — every discrepancy carries source-row IDs ④ Exception classification (Unbilled dispatches / Quantity & rate variances / Timing lags & returns) ⑤ One-page executive summary ⑥ 30-minute management walkthrough ⑦ Fixed-price automation roadmap.
3. **Scope boundaries (the "fair fixed price" table):** included — 1 workflow, 2 workbooks, ≤100k rows, 4 business days; **not included** — manual data entry from paper, mid-pilot schema changes, live ERP write-backs, extra workflows (each additional workflow = separate add-on quote).
4. **Fee & terms:** ₹8,500 + GST · 100% upfront before file intake · **credited 100%** toward implementation if signed within 14 days · delivery clock starts when clean agreed inputs arrive (the 24-hour feasibility gate, explained honestly: *"if files arrive corrupted or missing join keys, we pause and tell you — we don't silently burn the SLA"*).
5. **What happens if we find nothing:** explicit commitment — a clean report is a valid result; you still get the reconciled master dataset and documented matching rules. *(This honesty pre-empts "guarantee savings" objections and matches your SOW.)*
6. **Security summary + link to /security.**
7. **CTA:** [Book a 15-minute call] + [Download sample exception register].

### 4.3 HOW IT WORKS `/how-it-works`

The 7-step engagement journey (from the MVP roadmap): ① 20-min qualification call ② NDA + fixed-scope SOW via e-sign ③ secure file intake + 24-hour feasibility check ④ automated pipeline run (local, deterministic matching) ⑤ manual QA — top-10 variances and random-match verification, control totals reconciled ⑥ 30-min executive findings walkthrough ⑦ your decision window: implementation sprint, retainer, or done. Plus payment terms (advance before intake; implementation 40/40/20 milestones), turnaround SLAs per service, and the revision policy (one consolidated revision round, 5 business days).

### 4.4 DATA SECURITY `/security` (the page that closes Wall 2)

1. The five guarantees (offline air-gapped processing on encrypted drives · NDA before files · zero public AI/cloud · read-only ingestion, originals never modified · cryptographic deletion 14 days post-handover with written confirmation on request).
2. **How data travels:** what we ask you to send (exports, not logins; masked samples welcome), accepted channels, what we never ask for (bank OTPs, live Tally credentials, passwords — ever).
3. **Data minimization:** we request only the columns needed for the agreed scope.
4. **Our legal posture:** mutual NDA (1 page, plain English), SOW with liability capped at fees paid, and the statutory-neutrality disclaimer.
5. Download: the blank 1-page NDA (PDF) so prospects can read it *before* the call — a pre-call trust accelerator no competitor offers.

### 4.5 PROOF `/proof`

- **Sample exception register** (interactive/downloadable, synthetic): the 5 engineered scenarios — exact match, unbilled dispatch >5 days, quantity shortfall, timing lag, duplicate challan reference — color-coded, with source-row IDs visible.
- **60-second walkthrough video** (Loom embed): messy folder → one-click refresh → exception tab → totals reconcile.
- **One-page sample executive summary** (PDF).
- Banner on every asset: *"Demonstration prototype built on synthetic data. Client files are never shown publicly."*
- **No testimonials, no client logos, no invented case studies** until real, written-approved ones exist (Phase 2). An empty-but-honest proof page outperforms a fake one; sophisticated buyers smell fabrication instantly.

### 4.6 WHO WE HELP `/who-we-help`

Three anchored sections, each with: their workflow, the specific reconciliation failure modes, and the matching entry offer.
- **Distributors & stockists** (primary): dispatch-to-invoice gaps, bin card vs Tally, unallocated customer receipts, dead stock. Offer: dispatch-to-invoice diagnostic.
- **SME manufacturers** (secondary): Tally vs shop-floor/job-work logs, subcontractor scrap vs BOM norms, PO–GRN–invoice matching. Offer: production-to-finance reconciliation (note: scoped after discovery — BOM rules must be agreed first).
- **CA & bookkeeping firms** (channel): white-label client data cleanup and pre-audit scrubbing at wholesale per-client rates; link to the partner NDA. Explicit line: *"We work behind your brand; we never contact your clients."*
- City cluster mentions for local relevance and SEO (Gandhipuram, Ram Nagar, Kurichi, Peelamedu; Sowcarpet, George Town, Ambattur, Guindy; Karur textile belt).

### 4.7 PRICING `/pricing`

The offer ladder table with: what's included, delivery time, price (diagnostic = exact ₹8,500 + GST; sprint and retainer = "from" ranges pending scoping), payment terms, the credit mechanism, and the change-order policy (out-of-scope work billed at ₹1,200/hr only with signed change orders). A short "why fixed price" note: fixed price is possible because scope is frozen — and if your data can't support a fixed price, we tell you at the feasibility gate instead of surprising you.

### 4.8 FAQ `/faq`

Structure each as **Objection → short answer → link for detail**. Mandatory entries: "Tally already does bank reconciliation — what exactly do you do?" (cross-system: challans/gate logs/marketplace/bank UTRs vs Tally — what Tally can't see) · "My accountant/CA already handles this" (they do statutory compliance; we audit the operational gap they never see — and we make their audit faster) · "We can do this in Excel ourselves" (yes — the question is who spends 3 days a month on VLOOKUPs, and what happens when that person is on leave) · "Can you guarantee you'll find leaked money?" (no — we guarantee 100% auditable traceability; a clean result is a real result) · "Is my data safe?" (→ /security) · "Why ₹8,500 and not hourly?" (fixed scope, fixed price; hourly rewards slowness) · "What if my files are a mess?" (that's the job — but the feasibility gate exists so messiness is priced before commitment, not after) · "Do you replace Tally?" (never — read-only, Tally stays your system of record).

### 4.9 ABOUT `/about`

Founder story (data engineering background, why distributor reconciliation), operating principles (evidence over enthusiasm; offline by default; scope frozen before price fixed), and **what we don't do** (statutory audit, tax filing, ERP replacement, bookkeeping). Solo-practitioner honesty is an asset here: *"You work directly with the person who builds your pipeline."*

### 4.10 CONTACT `/contact`

Calendly embed (15-min slot) · short form (name, business, city, one question: *"Which two records in your business refuse to agree?"* — a qualifying question that filters casual traffic) · consent checkbox for contact per DPDPA · phone/WhatsApp policy line (*"WhatsApp answered for scheduling only"* — TRAI/DPDP-safe) · response SLA: within 1 business day.

### 4.11 PRIVACY + LEGAL SUMMARY

Privacy policy: what is collected via the site (form data, analytics), purpose, consent & withdrawal, retention, DPDPA rights, contact. Legal summary page: plain-English version of NDA/SOW/liability-cap/non-audit disclaimer. Footer disclaimer on every page: *"Deliverables are internal management decision-support aids. We are not CAs/CPAs and provide no statutory audit, tax, or legal opinions."*

## 5. COPY & CONTENT RULES

1. **Outcome-first always.** Every headline sells a business result; tools appear only in explanation sections.
2. **Vocabulary mining loop:** after every 5 discovery interviews, inject 2–3 verbatim prospect phrases into the problem section and FAQ. The site copy should sound like your buyers, not like a SaaS template.
3. **Numbers with honesty:** show ranges and "from" pricing where scoping is required; never publish savings guarantees or invented client results (Stage 15 rule: no invented traction — this applies to the website more than anywhere, because the website is public).
4. **No "AI-powered."** Sell *deterministic accuracy and offline privacy*; the AI-assist stays invisible in your internal workflow.
5. **Reading level:** simple sentences; the owner reads on a phone between meetings. Tamil one-line tagline optional; English primary.
6. **Every page ends in one CTA.** Never two competing asks.
