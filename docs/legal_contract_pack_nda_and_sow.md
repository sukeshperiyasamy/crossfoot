# Legal Contract Pack: Mutual NDA, Pilot SOW & Liability Disclaimer

This document contains your complete, plain-English legal kit drafted for commercial enforceability under the **Indian Contract Act, 1872**, the **Information Technology Act, 2000** (for global e-signatures), and cross-border B2B norms across the US, UK, and Australia.

All three documents are intentionally free of dense legalese so SME owners, Managing Directors, and Finance Heads can read and sign them in under five minutes. Simply replace the `[Bracketed Text]` with your business details and export as clean PDFs or send via Zoho Sign / DocuSign.

*(Each a **"Party"** and collectively the **"Parties"**).*

### 1. Purpose
The Parties wish to explore and execute data consolidation, spreadsheet automation, and management reporting services (the **"Purpose"**). In doing so, either Party may share confidential business or technical information with the other.

### 2. What Counts as Confidential Information
**"Confidential Information"** means any non-public data or material shared in any format (including `.xlsx`, `.csv`, `.xml`, `.json`, PDF, screen-share, or verbal disclosure), specifically including:

* **Client Data:** Sales registers, accounting exports (Tally, QuickBooks, Xero, ERP), bank statements, customer and vendor lists, SKU-level costing, profit margins, inventory records, and tax identifiers.
* **Consultant Materials:** Proprietary Python scripts, Power Query (`M`) code templates, data-cleaning architectures, pricing proposals, and diagnostic methodologies.

*Exclusions:* Confidential Information does not include data that is publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party, already lawfully known prior to disclosure, independently developed without using the disclosing Party's data, or required to be disclosed by law or court order (with prompt prior notice where legally permitted).

### 3. Strict Protection & Need-to-Know Access
Each Party agrees to: (a) use the other Party's Confidential Information strictly for the Purpose; (b) protect it using industry-standard security (including encrypted storage and password-protected devices); and (c) never sell, publish, or disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.

### 4. Offline Processing & Zero Public AI Guarantee
Consultant explicitly warrants that:

* **Local Deterministic Execution:** All Client financial ledgers, spreadsheets, and operational files will be processed locally/offline using deterministic tools (such as Python, Microsoft Excel Power Query, SQL, or Power BI Desktop) unless cloud hosting is explicitly requested by Client in writing.
* **Zero Public AI Uploads:** Consultant will **never** upload, paste, or transmit raw Client financial records, customer/vendor names, or confidential spreadsheets into any public, third-party generative AI model (such as public ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini web interfaces) or permit Client data to be used to train external AI models without Client's express prior written consent.

### 5. Data Retention & Guaranteed Deletion
Unless extended by an active monthly retainer agreement, Consultant will retain raw Client files for no longer than **fourteen (14) calendar days** following project handover solely to support post-delivery verification. Upon expiration of those 14 days—or immediately upon Client's written request at any time—Consultant will permanently delete all raw Client datasets and working copies from local drives and backups, and confirm deletion in writing upon request.

### 6. Term, Electronic Signatures & Governing Law
This Agreement remains in effect for **two (2) years** from the Effective Date, and confidentiality obligations regarding financial and customer records shall survive for two (2) years following termination. Electronic signatures (including PDF signatures, DocuSign, Zoho Sign, or email acceptance) carry the same legal force as ink signatures. This Statement of Work (**"SOW"**) incorporates the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between the Parties and sets the exact scope, timeline, and terms for the Data Diagnostic Pilot.

### 1. Pilot Objective & Fixed Deliverable Scope
Within **four (4) business days** of receiving clean, accessible sample exports and advance payment, Consultant will deliver:

1. **Data Health & Leakage Audit (PDF/Sheet):** Automated profiling identifying duplicate records, formatting mismatches, missing keys, and reconciliation gaps across the provided files.

3. **Executive Summary View:** **One (1) interactive summary dashboard** (delivered in Microsoft Excel or Power BI Desktop) highlighting up to **five (5) core KPIs** agreed at kickoff, plus one **Automated Exception Tab** flagging anomalous rows.
4. **Handover Walkthrough:** One 20-minute live call (or recorded screen-share walkthrough) demonstrating the findings and presenting a fixed-price roadmap for full-scale automation.

### 2. Explicit Out-of-Scope Exclusions (Guardrails)
To deliver rapid, fixed-cost results in 4 days, this Pilot strictly **excludes**:

* **Manual Data Entry:** Typing PDF/paper invoices, fixing human spelling errors row-by-row in source systems, or ongoing clerical bookkeeping.
* **Mid-Pilot Schema Changes:** Altering source column headers, adding extra workbooks/tabs beyond the initial 2 files, or changing KPI formulas after Pilot Day 1 kickoff.
* **Live ERP Write-Backs:** All work is strictly **read-only** on exported copies; Consultant never writes, edits, or deletes live records inside Client's Tally, QuickBooks, Xero, Shopify, or banking systems.

## Quick Operational Checklist Before Sending Your First Contract

- [ ] **Bundle Into One Single 3-Page PDF:** Combine Document 1 (Page 1), Document 2 (Page 2), and Document 3 (Page 3) into a single clean PDF when pitching a brand-new client so they only have to click **"Sign"** twice in one session rather than chasing three separate email attachments.
- [ ] **Use Zero-Friction E-Signatures:** Upload the PDF to **Zoho Sign** (Indian servers, legally compliant under the IT Act 2000, generous free/low-cost tier) or **DocuSign / PandaDoc**. Both domestic Indian proprietors and US/UK founders sign browser links on their phones 3x faster than printing, stamping, and scanning PDFs.
- [ ] **Cross Out the Unused Currency Before Sending:** Never send dual pricing (`₹8,500 / $250`) on a live SOW. Put **`₹8,500 INR`** for Indian prospects and **`$250 USD`** (or `£200 GBP` / `$380 AUD`) for international prospects so foreign buyers never ask for domestic rupee pricing.
- [ ] **Verify Payment Rail Details:** Ensure the payment instructions match your dedicated current account or cross-border payment profile (Skydo/Wise) with clear invoicing terms.
